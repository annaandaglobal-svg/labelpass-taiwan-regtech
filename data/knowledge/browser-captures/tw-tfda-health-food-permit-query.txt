Public registry of licensed 健康食品 (Health Food) products. Columns: 項次 / 許可證字號 (permit no., 衛署健食規字 = spec-standard route, or 衛署健食字 = individual-review route) / 中文品名 / 保健功效 (approved health efficacy) / 申請商 (applicant).
Sample records (approved efficacy shown):
- 衛署健食規字第000001號 濟生活力深海魚油膠囊 — 調節血脂 (fish oil, blood-lipid regulation)
- 衛署健食規字第000004號 增養紅麴膠囊 — 調節血脂 (紅麴 / red yeast rice, blood-lipid regulation)
- 衛署健食規字第000005號 台糖紅麴膠囊 — 調節血脂 (紅麴)
- 衛署健食規字第000010號 雅芳康采紅麴膠囊 — 調節血脂 (紅麴)
Key point: to make a health claim (e.g. 調節血脂 blood-lipid regulation) in Taiwan, a food must hold a 健康食品 permit (健康食品管理法). Red yeast rice (紅麴) products with blood-lipid claims are registered here. Plain foods cannot carry these claims without the permit.
