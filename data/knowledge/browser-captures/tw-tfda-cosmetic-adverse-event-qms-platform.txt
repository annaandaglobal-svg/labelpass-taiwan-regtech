Captured via browser.

TFDA post-market (上市後) quality management / adverse-event reporting portal covering 食品 (food), 藥品 (drugs), 醫療器材 (medical devices), and 化粧品 (cosmetics).
- 化粧品不良事件通報 (cosmetic adverse-event reporting), plus 藥品不良品通報 / 藥品療效不等通報 / 醫療器材不良事件通報.
- Vendor login requires an E-government account (E政府帳號); manufacturer/importer account application ("藥品醫療器材及化粧品廠商登入帳號申請須知").
- Mobile quick-reporting supported (no download, barcode scan, photo upload).
Reporting hotlines: 化粧品通報 02-2521-5027; 食品通報 02-2321-0594; 廠商帳號(化粧品) 02-2787-7515.
Relevance: Taiwan cosmetics post-market obligation — importers/responsible firms must report serious adverse events here after a product is on market (化粧品衛生安全管理法 post-market duties).
