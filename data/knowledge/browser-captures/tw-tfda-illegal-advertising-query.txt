TFDA public query of advertisements ruled ILLEGAL for food, drugs, cosmetics, and medical devices (違規廣告). Used to check which health/efficacy advertising claims have been penalised — a reference for what label/marketing wording is NOT permitted in Taiwan.
Relevance: food and cosmetics must avoid medical/therapeutic efficacy claims (誇大不實 / 醫療效能) that violate 食品安全衛生管理法 or 化粧品衛生安全管理法. This registry lists real penalised cases as guidance on prohibited advertising expressions.
